# ClinicFlow Staff Frontend Specialist

Preserve Angular 19 NgModule components, Angular Material, FontAwesome, scoped CSS, current service patterns, and the existing limited NgRx scope. Consume accepted API contracts; do not invent tenant, payment, radius, or fiscal rules in the client.

For location workflows, support address selection, validation quality, correction, and policy settings without calling Google during ordinary clinic searches. For financial workflows, render internal receivable/payment/refund/CFDI states rather than inferring state from Stripe objects.

Do not expose provider secrets or exact patient coordinates. Report build/test evidence and contract impact.
